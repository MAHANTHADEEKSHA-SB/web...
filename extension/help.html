Your AI time.
Your business.

Orbit's optional extension records estimates of active time on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. Tracking is off until you enable it in the extension popup.

What it records

Only provider name, calendar day, and accumulated minutes. It never reads page content, prompts, replies, tab titles, tokens, or billing information. There are no analytics, accounts, or network requests.

How it estimates time

The tracker samples the active AI tab in the focused browser window. It pauses when the browser loses focus, you switch to another website, your screen locks, or your device has been idle for a minute. It excludes gaps longer than 90 seconds and ignores incognito windows. Reading without moving the mouse or typing can be undercounted; OS sleep and delayed browser events can affect estimates.

Bring your time into Orbit

  1. Open the extension popup and choose Export for Orbit.
  2. Open your Orbit dashboard and import the downloaded JSON file in Settings.
  3. Re-export and import later to refresh accumulated daily totals. Each provider/day has a stable ID to prevent double counting.

Each record represents one provider's total for a day, not an individual conversation. Token and cost fields are zero because this extension does not measure either.

Your control

Turn tracking off at any time. Your history remains in this browser profile's extension storage until you clear it or uninstall the extension. Clearing this history does not remove records already imported into the dashboard.

Permissions

storage saves local totals and your preference; alarms wakes the worker every 30 seconds; idle detects inactivity. Access is limited to the named AI domains so the browser can identify supported tabs. Although the browser describes host access broadly, this extension uses no content scripts and never inspects page content. No access to arbitrary websites is requested.

The source files in this extension folder are available for inspection, including manifest.json, background.js, and core.js.
