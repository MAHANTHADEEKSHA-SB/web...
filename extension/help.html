Orbit 1.1.0 records active time and observed send actions on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. Enable Track AI time & sends in the popup to start. Tracking starts paused and pauses again after extension updates.

What it records

Provider name, calendar day, active minutes, and observed send counts. A content script watches send controls and inspects their structure and attributes. It does not read or store prompt values or replies, scan conversation history, or collect tab titles. There are no analytics, accounts, or network requests.

What an observed send means

Recognized send-button clicks, Enter presses, and form submissions count as observed actions. Related events within 750 milliseconds are combined; event IDs prevent duplicate deliveries from adding another count. These are not provider-confirmed successful messages. Failed attempts can be counted, changed layouts or languages can be missed, and very rapid actions can be combined. Confirm detection on the services you use.

Orbit cannot read official token usage, billing, subscription credits, or remaining message limits. It does not calculate these from your send counts or time. Manual token and cost entries in the dashboard remain separate from browser observations.

How time is recorded

The tracker samples the active AI tab in the focused browser window. It pauses when you switch away, the browser loses focus, your screen locks, or your device is idle for a minute. It excludes gaps longer than 90 seconds and ignores incognito windows. Reading without moving the mouse or typing can be undercounted; OS sleep and delayed events can affect timing.

Bring your activity into Orbit

  1. Choose Export for Orbit in the extension popup.
  2. Import the downloaded JSON file in your Orbit dashboard's Settings.
  3. Repeat to refresh totals. Matching provider/day IDs are replaced, not duplicated.

Each record is a daily provider total, not a conversation. Exports identify their source as extension. Send counts are numeric where observed; old records without counts have null. Tokens and costs are also null because they are unavailable, not zero.

Updating an older installation

  1. Download the new ZIP from Orbit Settings and replace files in the same extension folder.
  2. Click Reload on Orbit in your browser's Extensions page.
  3. Refresh your AI tabs and enable Track AI time & sends again.

Existing time records remain saved. Send counts start when the updated observer is enabled; past conversations are not counted. Keep the same extension installed and folder location to retain its local history.

Your control and permissions

Turn tracking off at any time. History remains in this browser profile until cleared or the extension is uninstalled. Clearing extension history does not remove records already imported into the dashboard.

storage saves your choice, daily totals, and short-lived deduplication IDs; alarms samples time every 30 seconds; idle detects inactivity. Host access and content scripts cover only the named AI sites. Google Search, unrelated websites, mobile apps, and desktop AI apps are not tracked. There is no broad tabs permission. Keep incognito access disabled.

Source files are available in this extension folder, including manifest.json, content.js, background.js, and core.js.
